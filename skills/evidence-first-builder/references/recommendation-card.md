# Recommendation Card

Use for consequential choices. Keep it compact and omit fields that truly do not apply.

## Default card

- **Decision:** chosen option or recommendation
- **Why:** 1-3 decisive fit reasons
- **Evidence:** strongest primary evidence plus the most useful independent/reality-check evidence
- **Weaknesses / conflicts:** important downside, conflicting evidence, or missing capability
- **Commercial-bias check:** sponsorship/affiliate/SEO/vendor-promotion risk and how it was neutralized
- **Open-source / lower-cost alternative:** best serious alternative when relevant
- **Verification:** `VERIFIED_IN_TARGET`, `IMPLEMENTATION_PASS`, `LIVE_ACCEPTANCE_PENDING`, `RESEARCH_ONLY`, or other precise status
- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low; explain briefly when not High

## Comparison variant

When 2-4 candidates remain genuinely viable, use one compact table or short cards. Compare only decision-changing dimensions such as compatibility, evidence strength, maintenance, privacy, cost/lock-in, and verification.

Do not fill a large matrix with trivial attributes just to look comprehensive.
